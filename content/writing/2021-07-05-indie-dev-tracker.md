Initially a Twitter bot written in Python (using [Birdcall](/writing/birdcall)) that retweets quality content (especially screenshots), runs community threads, and more.

Naturally, Indie Dev Tracker evolved into a thriving game development community and moved to Discord. The bot itself now spans both platforms, creating content and engaging with the community while leveraging the generative power of artificial intelligence.
